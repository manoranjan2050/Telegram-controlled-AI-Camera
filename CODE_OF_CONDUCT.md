# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TelegramP4 to be welcoming to everyone learning embedded systems, IoT, and
AI — from school students to professional embedded developers. We pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and patient with beginners
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the learning goals of the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional/educational setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
by contacting the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
